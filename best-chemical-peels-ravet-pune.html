What to Expect from Chemical Peels

A chemical peel is one of the safest and most effective ways to deeply resurface the skin. By applying a medically formulated solution, we gently peel away the dull, damaged top layer of skin.

At SkinDoc Clinic, we customize the strength of the peel to match your exact skin type and concern:

  • Tan & Pigmentation Removal: Fades dark spots, melasma, and heavy sun tan.
  • Acne Marks: Accelerates the fading of post-breakout redness and dark scars.
  • Skin Texture: Evens out rough patches for a smooth, glowing finish.
  • Safe & Effective: We perform comprehensive patch tests prior to treatment to ensure no adverse reactions.
